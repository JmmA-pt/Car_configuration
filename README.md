# Dynamic Kinematrix

**An interactive 3D powertrain & engine simulator.**

Whether you’re tuning a naturally aspirated flat-six, stress-testing a twin-charged dyno build, or dissecting how high-voltage EV drivetrains deliver instant torque, **Dynamic Kinematrix** lets you assemble, crank, rev, and hear mechanical engineering come alive.

---

## Three Powertrain Worlds

- **ICE (Pure Combustion)** — For the mechanical purists. Configure inline, V, flat/boxer, and Wankel rotary layouts alongside custom valvetrains, forced induction, and exhaust headers—watching every piston, cam lobe, and apex seal move in sync.
- **Hybrid** — Where combustion character meets electric fill. Pair an internal combustion core with electric motors and high-voltage battery packs to see power-split dynamics in real time.
- **100% Electric** — High-efficiency, instant-response propulsion. Experiment with motor topologies, rotor designs, gear reductions, and battery configurations under live load.

---

## Core Features

- **X-Ray 3D Inspection** — Orbit, zoom, toggle transparent cutaway housings, and engage slow-motion to inspect internal kinematics from idle to redline.
- **Procedural Engine & Motor Audio** — Real-time synthesized sound shaped by firing order, cylinder count, induction whine, exhaust resonance, and inverter/motor pitch.
- **Live Dyno & Telemetry** — Rev the throttle under load and track real-time horsepower, torque curves, RPM, and thermal/mechanical response.
*(Put on headphones for the best audio experience.)*
